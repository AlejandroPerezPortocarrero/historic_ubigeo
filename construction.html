How the datasets are built

Goal

ubigeocensos records the presence of each department, province and district of Peru in the national population censuses of 1981, 1993, 2007, 2017 and 2025, together with each unit’s UBIGEO code and its date and legal instrument of creation. The aim is to make it easier to link census data across years, for example when building district panels that have to account for newly created districts.

The reference frame is the political-administrative division of the 2025 Census. The 2025 INEI UBIGEO is the unique identifier.

The datasets are built by data-raw/make_datasets.R, which runs the scripts in data-raw/ in order.

INEI (1999): Directorio Nacional de Municipalidades

Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática (1999). Perú: Directorio Nacional de Municipalidades Provinciales y Distritales 1999. Lima: INEI. https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/web/biblioineipub/bancopub/Est/Lib0362/indice.htm

For each department the publication has a table titled “Dispositivo legal de creación, población total, superficie, densidad poblacional y población electoral de los distritos: 1999”. For Lima it is table 15.3 (https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/web/biblioineipub/bancopub/Est/Lib0362/dis-lima.htm), and the other departments follow the pattern dis-<code>.htm (dis-amaz.htm, dis-anca.htm, …, dis-ucay.htm).

For each district, the table reports:

  • the type of legal instrument (Ley, Decreto, Decreto Ley, Ley Regional), its number and its date;
  • EPOCA INDEP. for districts that date from the Independence era and have no identified instrument of creation;
  • total population at 30/06/1998, surface (km²), density and electoral population at 04/01/1999.

Scope. The table covers only districts that existed in 1999. Districts created afterwards need another source, such as the laws published in El Peruano or the PCM’s territorial demarcation records.

Step 1: units in the 2025 Census

Script: data-raw/01_units_2025.R.

sapply(list(departments = departments, provinces = provinces, districts = districts), nrow)
#> departments   provinces   districts 
#>          25         196        1892

Checks run by the script:

  • UBIGEO codes are unique;
  • every district has its province and every province has its department;
  • no province is left without districts.

Step 2: creation dates (INEI 1999)

Script: data-raw/02_inei1999_creation.R. The script reads the text copied from each dis-<code>.htm, turns it into a table and matches it to the 2025 UBIGEO by department, province and normalised name (upper case, no accents, keeping the Ñ). It stops with an error if any district is left unmatched.

do.call(rbind, lapply(split(inei1999_districts, inei1999_districts$dept), function(d)
  data.frame(dept = d$dept[1], districts = nrow(d), matched = sum(!is.na(d$inei)),
             independence_era = sum(d$independence_era),
             with_date = sum(!is.na(d$created_date)))))
#>    dept districts matched independence_era with_date
#> 15   15       171     171               52       119

Notes on the source

Values that look wrong are kept as published and flagged in the note column:

subset(inei1999_districts, !is.na(note),
       c(inei, district_1999, instrument_number, date_text, note))
#>      inei            district_1999 instrument_number   date_text
#> 88 150612 VEINTISIETE DE NOVIEMBRE             17702 18 ENE 1957

Districts by period of creation (Lima)

lima <- subset(districts, department == "LIMA")
period <- with(lima, ifelse(independence_era, "Independence era",
               ifelse(created_date < as.Date("1900-01-01"), "1821-1899",
               ifelse(created_date < as.Date("1950-01-01"), "1900-1949",
               ifelse(created_date < as.Date("1981-07-12"), "1950-1981 census",
                      "After the 1981 census")))))
table(period)
